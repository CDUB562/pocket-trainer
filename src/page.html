Help

No sound

Turn the volume up with the side buttons (sound plays even with the silent switch on, on iOS 17 and later). If it's still quiet, close the app fully and open it again. Using your own recording? Tap "Use built-in click" to rule it out.

The voice guide says No signal

Each skill's voice is downloaded the first time you listen. Get a connection and tap Listen again, or save the whole guide above before you head out.

The sound comes late

Use the phone speaker. Bluetooth speakers and AirPods add up to 200 ms, enough to mark the wrong moment. Low Power Mode can also slow it a little.

No vibration

iPhone vibration on taps needs iOS 18 and System Haptics on (Settings, Sounds & Haptics). It's a bonus; the sound is what your dog learns from.

Record does nothing or the camera is blocked

Allow the camera: Settings, Apps, Safari, Camera, Ask or Allow. Then close the app and open it again. Filming stops if you leave the app or lock the phone.

My dog isn't getting it

Go back a step (‹ Easier), train in a quieter room, use better treats and keep sessions to 2 or 3 minutes. Aim for 8 or more rewards a minute. If Yes doesn't make your dog look up, do another round of Charge Yes.

My progress is gone

Progress lives on this phone only, separately in Safari and in the installed app. Clearing Safari's website data or deleting the app erases it. Save a backup now and then, below.

    How Yes works

    Yes is a camera shutter. It tells your dog the exact moment they earned the treat, so they can repeat that moment. The treat comes a second later; the sound is the part they learn from.

    The rules

    1. 1Charge it first.Before Yes means anything, it has to mean food. Do the 2-minute Charge routine once or twice, then start training.
    2. 2Tap the instant it happens.Mark when the rear touches the floor, not when the dog is already getting up again. Every step tells you exactly what to mark.
    3. 3Every Yes gets a treat.Even an accidental one. A Yes without food teaches your dog to ignore it.
    4. 4Hands still, then treat.Tap first, then reach for the treat. If your hand moves first, your dog watches your hand instead of listening.
    5. 5Yes ends the rep.After Yes your dog may move to get the treat. That's fine. Set up the next rep, or tap Free to finish.
    6. 6No Yes for nothing.Don't use it to call your dog, to get attention, or as praise. Praise all you want, but with your voice.
    7. 7Missed it? Tap Miss.No sound, no treat, no scolding. Just reset and try again. Two misses in a row means the step is too hard: make it easier.

    When to use Yes

    Use it while you're teaching or polishing a skill. Once a step is solid (4 of 5 right on at least two different days, in more than one place), you can reward with praise, play, or a door opening instead, and save Yes for new steps. Whenever you do use Yes, food follows.

    The four sounds

    YesThat was it. Come get your treat from my hand.
    CatchThat was it, and the treat is flying. Toss it so your dog moves away and resets for the next rep.
    Keep goingYou're on the right track, keep doing it. No treat. It only shows on holds and walking steps.
    FreeYou're released. The session is over, go sniff.

    The recall whistle

    1. 1The blue Recall button plays it from any screen. Use it only to call your dog, never to scold or interrupt.
    2. 2Every time it plays, pay a jackpot: 5 or more treats in a row, or the best toy.
    3. 3Charge it at home first: play it, then jackpot, ten times over a few days, before you ever need it outside.
