Pocket Trainer privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026. Applies to the Pocket Trainer iPhone app and the web app at this address.

The short version

Pocket Trainer collects nothing. Everything you put into it stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • Your dog's name, training progress, settings and practice log are saved on your device only.
  • Videos you film are saved in the app on your device. They are never uploaded. You can share or save one yourself from the review screen.
  • If you record your own "Yes" sound, that short recording is saved on your device only.
  • Backups are files you choose to save, for example to Files or iCloud Drive. Where they go is up to you.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you tap Record, to film a training session for your own review. The microphone is used only when you tap Record my own Yes. Neither runs in the background, and nothing they capture leaves your device unless you share it.

Deleting your data

Settings, Erase everything removes all progress, settings, videos and recordings. Deleting the app removes everything too.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue or see the support page.
