Pocket Trainer support

A dog training clicker and coach for your phone. These answers are also in the app under Settings, Help.

No sound

Turn the volume up with the side buttons. Sound plays even with the silent switch on. If it's still quiet, close the app fully and open it again. Using your own recording? Switch back to the built-in click in Settings to rule it out.

The sound comes late

Use the phone speaker. Bluetooth speakers and AirPods add up to 200 ms of delay, enough to mark the wrong moment. The training screen warns you when Bluetooth is connected.

No vibration

Check that "Vibrate on every mark" is on in the app's Settings, and that System Haptics is on in the iPhone's Settings, Sounds & Haptics.

Record does nothing

Allow the camera in the iPhone's Settings, Pocket Trainer, Camera. Filming stops if you leave the app or lock the phone; what was filmed is kept.

My dog isn't getting it

Go back a step (Easier), train in a quieter room, use better treats and keep sessions to 2 or 3 minutes. Aim for 8 or more rewards a minute. If Yes doesn't make your dog look up, do another round of Charge Yes.

Moving to a new phone

In Settings, Save a backup and keep it in Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, use Restore a backup. Videos and your recorded Yes are not included.

Still stuck?

Open an issue describing what happened and your iPhone model. See also the privacy policy.
